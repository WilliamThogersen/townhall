08 principles

  • 01
    Conversations first. You talk, townhall finds the repo and turns the work into tasks that agents pick up. There is no other way to start an agent.
  • 02
    Color means state. The chrome is black, white and grey. Green, amber, red and blue only ever say something about an agent, a step or a message.
  • 03
    Amber means you. Your messages, your focus, your cursor, the pane you are in, now on the timeline, and anything waiting on you.
  • 04
    One font, lines not boxes. Monospace everywhere, 1px lines, square corners, no shadows except on things that float.
  • 05
    Quiet until it matters. Nothing blinks or pulses. The working spinner is the only animation. What needs you sorts to the top.

09 color

Hex values are read live from tokens.css, so they follow the theme toggle. Every text pair passes WCAG AA (node docs/style-guide/check-contrast.mjs). The heat ramp is one hue, empty to full, with its own steps per theme.
